Constraints: produce one finished coherent square collage, not a set of options. Flat edge-to-edge artwork, no mockup frame, no title outside the collage, no watermark. Avoid robot heads, humanoid robots, glowing digital brains, circuit boards, network node webs, holograms, blue sci-fi imagery, tiny gibberish text, stock corporate infographics, glossy 3D icons, fake publisher logos, fake real-person quotations.

Primary request: A contemporary collage visualizing how AI saturates public discourse as an endless digital media feed. Design direction 2: ENDLOSER FEED.
Composition: 6 to 8 large overlapping, cropped fragments of believable 2026 digital media: a vertical social-video still with a person speaking into a microphone, a compact chat exchange, a news headline fragment, a video-call debate, a portrait subtly duplicated as synthetic media, and a classroom scene. Several pieces extend off the edges, creating the sense that the conversation continues everywhere beyond the frame. A clear visual hierarchy and readable space between key headlines; art-directed overload, not random clutter. Do not arrange as a tidy dashboard or bento grid.

Crisp typography, photographic detail, layered screen fragments with subtle depth. Visually fresh and human, not futuristic science-fiction. Flat artwork itself, not a photo of a phone or monitor.
Text, exact German phrases, each on a separate prominent fragment: 'KI im Job?', 'Echt oder generiert?', 'Wer entscheidet?', 'Lernen mit KI?', plus one large 'KI' and a small repeated 'KI' only where composition benefits. 3 – KI im Alltag
Datei: wtfi-0-ki-variante-3-alltag.png

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one square high-resolution image, ideally 2048 x 2048, for a university lecture slide about data science. It must be visually legible when displayed at half slide width.
Primary request: A refined photographic editorial collage conveying the omnipresence of AI in everyday society and public conversation. Design direction 3: KI IM ALLTAG.
Subject and composition: A compelling layered photomontage of contemporary human life: a commuter looking at a phone, two people debating across a cafe table, university students discussing work, a clinician at a monitor, a creative professional at a screen, and a public policy discussion with microphones. Build a continuous visual flow across 6 large overlapping photographic fragments rather than separate framed tiles. Vary scale and crop intelligently, with larger expressive faces and hands, a few glimpses of real workplaces and city life. Subtle clean graphic speech shapes and screen fragments recur across the images, linking the conversations. The viewer should feel AI is being discussed everywhere, even though no AI entity is depicted.
Style: sophisticated contemporary cultural magazine photographic montage, natural daylight, documentary-like photography of fictional people, warm realistic colors, strong black-white contrast with restrained coral and teal accents, clean digital masking and a few subtle translucent overlaps. Fresh, tactile and human, but absolutely not retro or vintage. The photographic content should be the dominant element.
Text: the only visible lettering is 'KI', repeated in 4 well-placed conversation/screen fragments of varying sizes, plus one prominent 'ÜBERALL' integrated into the composition. Precise spelling, elegant modern sans-serif. No fake newspaper articles, no tiny text.
Constraints: one finished square artwork, edge-to-edge, no external title, no watermark, no news brand logos. No robots, no glowing brains, no circuitry, no network diagrams, no central sci-fi AI sphere, no cybersecurity stock imagery. Human-centered, neither utopian nor dystopian. Cohesive collage, not a single continuous photographed scene and not a labeled infographic.
